1. CMCS — Chief Minister's Charter School

The Charter School concept, a Public-Private Partnership (PPP) model, allows a private entity to assume academic oversight of a public school. While remaining free and open to all students, the private organization gains a degree of autonomy in academic decision-making.

Sikkim established its inaugural Charter School, Pangthang JHS, in 2023. This achievement was the result of a collaborative network of organizations working toward a unified vision.

The genesis of the Charter model, inspired by successful international examples, was spearheaded by Ms. Kunzang Choeki, Founder-Director of the Katalytic Educational Foundation (KEF), in collaboration with leaders from partnering organizations. Ms. Choeki initially worked on the project independently with the other organizations before the formation of KEF in 2024. In the CMCS (Chief Minister's Charter School), KEF's primary function is to serve as the government liaison and coordinator among all participating organizations.

In the first year of the project, the kids reacted very positively to the changes and the literacy rates went up. Kids started to show an interest in reading and started enjoying their classes.

Through this project, we have also tried to bring in some public driven activities such as painting the school, where all interested people from Gangtok showed up with paint and brushes and decided to come together to paint the school for the kids.

2. Chief Minister's Mentorship Project (CMMP) / Vision X

The Vision X Project, also known as CMMP, is an initiative targeting over 5300 Grade X students in all 213 secondary and senior secondary public schools across Sikkim. The primary objective is to elevate the academic success of these students by significantly increasing the pass percentage in the Grade X CBSE board examinations.

The project involves a diverse group of stakeholders:

  • Students: 5300+ Grade X students.
  • Schools: 213 public secondary and senior secondary schools.
  • Educators: Over 1000 teachers specializing in English, Mathematics, Science, and Social Science (SST).
  • Leadership: More than 200 Heads of Schools (HoIs).

Launched in June 2025, the project commenced with in-person training sessions for all Heads of Institutions and subject teachers across the state's six districts.

3. Bisleri

KEF and Bisleri International have partnered to bring in awareness about sustainability in schools by implementing an activity based curriculum in charter schools. This strategic partnership is dedicated to fostering environmental consciousness and sustainable living within the school communities of Sikkim.

  • Mega Cleanliness Drives: Igniting community spirit through large-scale, student-led action across our charter schools. Conducted on 11 Dec 2025.
  • The "Bottle for Change" Campaign: Challenging over 200 secondary schools to reimagine plastic waste through innovation and upcycling.
  • The Integrated Curriculum: A 12-chapter academic journey titled "Towards Responsible Use of Plastic" for students at PJHS and PSAP. From understanding the science of degradation to drafting school-level environmental policies, students are empowered to become informed advocates for the planet.
