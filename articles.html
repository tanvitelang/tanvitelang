Concept note: Value-Guided Primary Education Curriculum

CONCEPT NOTE · FLN · NEP 2020

A curriculum model for primary education (Grades 1–3), starting with foundational literacy and numeracy (FLN). It is guided by core values such as empathy, critical thinking, responsibility and constitutional values. It builds on NEP 2020 and the NCF.
