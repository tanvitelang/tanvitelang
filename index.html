The short version

I did an integrated BS-MS in mathematics at IISER Bhopal, worked on a thesis under the supervision of Dr Angshuman Bhattacharya, and did a research year at the University of Houston under the supervision of Dr Mehrdad Kalantar, where I got introduced to hyperbolic groups, amenable groups and Furstenberg boundaries. I took a break from academia due to some personal reasons and went on to explore the corporate world and the education sector back home in Sikkim, India.
