I did an integrated BS-MS in mathematics at IISER Bhopal, wrote a thesis on C*-algebras, got selected for a summer school of 120 women mathematicians across India, and then spent a research year at the University of Houston studying operator algebras and hyperbolic groups. Somewhere in the middle of all that, I also learned to build data warehouses, audit enterprise systems, and put together the occasional 40-slide deck for a bank.
